The demo is Raven's November 1997 three-level Hexen II demo, fetched from archive.org and checked against known file hashes before it is installed. Hexenwail hosts no game data of its own.

Own Hexen II? Add your copy's data1/pak0.pak and pak1.pak for the full game, portals/pak3.pak for Portal of Praevus, music .ogg files, a .sf2 SoundFont, or a .zip of any of these. Or drop them anywhere on this page. Everything stays in this browser.

Hexenwail is a modernized Hexen II engine, built from Raven Software's GPL source, compiled to WebAssembly and rendered with WebGL2 in your browser. With no files of your own it plays Raven's free three-level demo of 1997. Install it from the browser's menu (on iPad, Share, Add to Home Screen) and it runs offline.
